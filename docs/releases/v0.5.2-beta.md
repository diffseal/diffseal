# Diffseal v0.5.2-beta

Configuration checks name their SOC 2 criterion. The analysis and the gate are unchanged.

## Changes

- Each configuration check names its SOC 2 criterion from the 2022 Trust Services Criteria, next to its ISO 27001 control: for example CC6.1 for encryption at rest, CC6.3 for least privilege and A1.2 for backups. It appears in the pull request comment, the job summary and the change record.
- The SQS dead-letter queue check no longer cites ISO 27001 A.8.14.

## Upgrade notes

1. Change `diffseal/diffseal@v0.5.1-beta` to `diffseal/diffseal@v0.5.2-beta`, and the reusable workflow to `diffseal/diffseal/.github/workflows/upload.yml@v0.5.2-beta`.
2. Change records keep their format. The evidence service reads records from older versions unchanged.
